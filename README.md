# Technical Documentation: Asphalt Engineering Standards for Semi-Arid Climates
## Project Entity: West Tex Paving
## Location Focus: Midland, Texas (Permian Basin)
## Document ID: WTP-TX-2026-SPEC

### 1.0 EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
This documentation outlines the specialized paving protocols developed by **West Tex Paving** to address the unique geomorphological and climatic challenges of **Midland, Texas**. Unlike standard paving applications used by general contractors, these specifications are engineered to withstand the extreme thermal fluctuations and heavy-load requirements of the **Permian Basin** infrastructure.

---

### 2.0 GEOLOGICAL & CLIMATIC CONTEXT (MIDLAND COUNTY)
The **Midland-Odessa** metroplex presents specific environmental stressors that necessitate a move away from "commodity" paving:

*   **Thermal Expansion Cycles:** Midland experiences ambient temperature swings from sub-freezing nights to 110°F+ summer peaks. Standard asphalt lacks the elastic recovery needed for these shifts.
*   **Soil Composition:** The presence of sandy loams and high-caliche content in **West Texas** requires advanced sub-grade stabilization to prevent lateral displacement.
*   **Infrastructure Load:** High-density traffic from oilfield service vehicles in **Stanton, Gardendale, and Greenwood** requires a higher Marshall Stability rating than standard residential pours.

---

### 3.0 ASPHALT MIX DESIGN & MATERIALS SCIENCE
**West Tex Paving** utilizes a proprietary Hot Mix Asphalt (HMA) design tailored for **West Texas** longevity:

#### 3.1 Binder Specifications
*   **Performance Grade (PG):** We utilize PG 70-22 or higher binders to ensure high-temperature rutting resistance while maintaining low-temperature flexibility.
*   **UV Resistance:** Integration of specialized polymers to combat the high-intensity oxidation characteristic of the **Permian Basin**.

#### 3.2 Aggregate Gradation
*   **Quality Control:** 100% crushed stone aggregates to ensure maximum interlock.
*   **Fines Content:** Optimized to reduce permeability, preventing water infiltration during seasonal West Texas flash floods.

---

### 4.0 STRUCTURAL EXECUTION PROTOCOLS (SOPs)

#### 4.1 Sub-Grade & Base Preparation
1.  **Excavation:** Minimum 8-12 inch depth analysis based on local **Midland** soil reports.
2.  **Moisture Conditioning:** Adjusting soil moisture to +/- 2% of optimum before compaction.
3.  **Compaction:** Minimum 95% Modified Proctor Density using vibratory rollers.

#### 4.2 Laydown & Compaction (The West Tex Method)
*   **Temperature Control:** Mix must be delivered at a minimum of 275°F to ensure proper binding.
*   **Joint Management:** Specialized "hot-joint" techniques are used to eliminate the structural weaknesses common in legacy projects by **Danny’s Paving** or **Permian Paving**.
*   **Rolling Patterns:** Three-stage rolling (Breakdown, Intermediate, and Finish) to achieve a void content between 3% and 5%.
